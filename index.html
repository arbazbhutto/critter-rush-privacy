Critter Rush

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

In short: Critter Rush does not ask for an account and does not collect personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID (see Section 4).

1. Who we are

Critter Rush ("the app", "the game") is a mobile game published by Arnu verse ("we", "us"). The app's package identifier is com.critter.rush. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect or store any personal information on our servers. The app:

  • has no sign-up, login or user account;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location;
  • does not include analytics tools of our own. However, our advertising partner described in Section 4 may collect certain device data;
  • does not record audio. The microphone is never used by the game.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • game progress: levels cleared, stars, best scores and number of runs;
  • in-game items: collected currency, outfits and achievements;
  • daily reward streak;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration and particle effects.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by resetting progress in the game's Settings, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising

Critter Rush shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob and its partners may collect and use the following data to show ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (such as model, operating system version and language);
  • ad interaction data (such as ad views and clicks).

We do not receive this data and we do not use it ourselves.

You can control ad personalization at any time: on your Android device go to Settings → Google → Ads, then choose "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID". Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

To learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases

The current version of the app has no real-money purchases. All in-game items are earned by playing.

6. Children's privacy

Critter Rush is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Third parties

We do not sell your personal data. The app uses Google AdMob (see Section 4), which operates under Google's own privacy policy. The app is also distributed through app stores (such as Google Play), which have their own privacy policies covering downloads and store activity.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at supportarnuverse@gmail.com.
